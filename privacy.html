This Privacy Policy explains what information Tekevol collects when you visit tekevol.com, fill out one of our forms, or book a call with us, and how we use and protect it. We keep it simple: we collect what we need to respond to you and run our business, and we don't sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Tekevol ("Tekevol," "we," "us," or "our") builds and manages websites for small businesses. This policy applies to tekevol.com and any forms or scheduling pages linked from it.

2. Information you give us

When you request a quote or contact us through our website, you may provide:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name and website URL
  • Your trade or industry
  • The service package you're interested in and whether you already have a website
  • Anything you include in your message

When you book a call through our scheduling page, we collect your name and email address, along with the date and time you choose.

If you email or call us directly, we'll keep a record of that conversation and the contact details you share.

3. Information collected automatically

When you browse our website, our analytics and CRM tools automatically collect certain information, such as:

  • Pages you visit and how long you spend on them
  • How you arrived at our site (for example, a search engine, social media, or a direct link)
  • Your browser, device type, and operating system
  • Your IP address and approximate location (city or region)

If you submit a form, the pages you viewed before and after submitting may be connected to your contact record so we understand what you were interested in.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. We use them to understand how people use our site and which pages lead to inquiries. We don't use cookies for advertising at this time.

Our service providers may also set a small number of technical cookies needed for security and performance.

Managing cookies: You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies won't stop you from using our site or submitting a form. To opt out of Google Analytics on all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your questions and quote requests
  • Schedule and hold calls with you
  • Prepare proposals and provide the services you sign up for
  • Send follow-up emails about your inquiry or project
  • Understand which pages and channels bring people to our site, so we can improve it
  • Prevent spam and misuse of our forms

If we send you marketing emails, every email will include an unsubscribe link.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed for them to do that work:

  • HubSpot stores our contact records, processes form submissions, tracks website visits, and runs our meeting scheduler. See HubSpot's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager help us measure site traffic and form submissions. We don't send your name, email, or phone number to Google Analytics. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Google Calendar and Google Meet are used to schedule and host calls you book with us.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a business sale or transfer.

7. Data retention

We keep contact information for as long as we need it to respond to you, work with you, and keep accurate business records. We periodically remove contacts we're no longer in touch with. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you
  • Correct information that's wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop sending you emails

To make a request, email support@tekevol.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

9. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" setting. There's no common industry standard for how websites should respond to it, so our site does not currently respond to Do Not Track signals. You can still control cookies using the options in the Cookies section.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and reputable service providers with their own security programs. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Links to other websites

Our site may link to other websites, such as our scheduling page or client sites we've built. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, so please review their policies.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we'll make that clear on our website.
